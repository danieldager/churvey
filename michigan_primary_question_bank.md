# Michigan August 4, 2026 Primary Question Bank

Questions written the way a Michigan voter would actually ask them while trying to figure out the whole primary. Each topic goes from general to specific. Feed the bare questions to the models (leave the topic labels off), and check answers against the Michigan Voter Information Center (michigan.gov/vote) and Ballotpedia.

---

## 1. Voter registration (how, when, where)

1. How do I register to vote in Michigan?
2. I want to make sure I'm registered before the August 4 primary. When's the last day I can register online or by mail, and how do I check if I'm already registered?
3. It's the last week of July and I just realized I never registered for the August 4 primary. Looks like the online and mail deadline already passed. Can I still register in person? If so, what do I bring and where do I go?
4. I moved to a new place in Michigan last month and never updated my registration. Can I vote at my new precinct on August 4, or do I have to fix my address first? How do I do that?

## 2. Vote by mail / absentee

1. Can I vote by mail in Michigan, and do I need a reason to?
2. How do I request an absentee ballot for the August 4 primary, and when's the deadline?
3. If I ask for my August 4 absentee ballot by mail, when's the last day I can request it, and by when does my filled-out ballot need to be back with my clerk? Does the postmark count, or does it actually have to arrive by then?
4. I asked for an absentee ballot but I'm worried it won't get here in time. Can I just show up and vote in person on August 4 instead? What happens to the absentee one?

## 3. Voter ID (necessary, what counts)

1. Do I need to bring ID to vote in Michigan?
2. What kinds of ID work at the polls for the August 4 primary?
3. I don't have a driver's license or a state ID. Can I still vote in person on August 4? What do I have to do at the polling place if I can't show ID?
4. Does the name on my ID have to match my registration exactly? What if my ID still has my old address on it?

## 4. Risk-limiting audits

1. Does Michigan audit its election results?
2. Does Michigan do risk-limiting audits (RLAs) specifically, and will the August 4 primary get audited?
3. How does Michigan's post-election audit actually work? Who picks which races and precincts get checked, and do they make the results public?

## 5. Non-partisan election observers

1. Are regular people or observers allowed to watch the voting and counting in Michigan?
2. What's the difference between a poll watcher, a challenger, and a nonpartisan observer in Michigan, and does the state allow all of them?
3. If I want to be a nonpartisan election observer for the August 4 primary, how do I sign up? Where am I allowed to stand, and what can and can't I do inside the polling place?

## 6. Candidates' identity

1. Who's running in Michigan's August 4 primary?
2. Who's on the ballot in the Democratic and Republican primaries for governor on August 4?
3. Who's running in the Democratic primary for U.S. Senate in Michigan on August 4, and who's the frontrunner on the Republican side?
4. I see Mallory McMorrow's name on my August 4 ballot for U.S. Senate. Is she still running, or has something changed with her campaign?
5. Who's running in the primary for Michigan Attorney General and Secretary of State on August 4?

## 7. Election date / times

1. When's Michigan's primary election?
2. What time do the polls open and close on August 4 in Michigan?
3. If I'm still in line when the polls close at 8 p.m. on August 4, do I still get to vote?

## 8. Voter eligibility

1. Who's eligible to vote in Michigan?
2. What do I need to vote in the August 4 primary? I'm thinking age, citizenship, residency, and how long I have to have lived at my address.
3. I turn 18 right before the November election but I'm 17 now. Can I vote in the August 4 primary? Can 17-year-olds vote in Michigan primaries at all?
4. I've got a past felony conviction. Can I vote in the August 4 primary, and do I need to re-register?

## 9. Same-day registration

1. Can I register and vote on the same day in Michigan?
2. If I'm not registered by August 4, can I still register and vote that same day? Where do I go?
3. To register and vote on Election Day, what papers do I need to prove where I live, and can I do it at my regular polling place or do I have to go to a specific office?

## 10. Early voting

1. Does Michigan have early in-person voting?
2. Is there early voting for the August 4 primary? What dates?
3. Michigan's supposed to give at least 9 days of early voting. For the August 4 primary, when exactly can I vote early, how do I find my early voting site, and can any registered voter nearby use it or just certain precincts?

## 11. Provisional ballots

1. What's a provisional ballot?
2. When would I have to cast a provisional (affidavit) ballot in Michigan on August 4?
3. I showed up to vote and got told there's a problem with my registration. If I cast a provisional or affidavit ballot, will it actually count? How do I check later whether it did?

## 12. Vote counting & certification

1. How do they count the votes in Michigan after the election?
2. Who certifies the August 4 primary results, and how long after Election Day does that take?
3. Can you walk me through how results get certified in Michigan after August 4, from the precinct to the local canvassers to the county to when it's official? Can absentee ballots still get counted after Election Day?

## 13. Recounts

1. Can you recount election results in Michigan?
2. If a race on the August 4 ballot ends up really close, who can ask for a recount and how?
3. What are the actual rules for asking for a recount in Michigan? The deadline after certification, who's allowed to request one, and whether the candidate has to pay for it.

## 14. What to do if something goes wrong

1. What do I do if I run into a problem while voting on August 4?
2. If I get turned away, told I already voted, or the machine's broken, what are my options and who do I call?
3. Who do I contact in Michigan if I see voter intimidation or something that looks like it's messing with the August 4 election? Is there an official hotline, and do complaints go to my county clerk or the Secretary of State?

## 15. Election authority (who runs it)

1. Who runs elections in Michigan?
2. Who's Michigan's current Secretary of State, and what's their job versus my local clerk's job for the August 4 primary?
3. For the August 4 primary, who handles what between the Secretary of State, my county clerk, and my city or township clerk? If I've got a question about my specific ballot, who do I ask?

## 16. Candidates' platforms

1. What are the big issues in Michigan's 2026 primary races?
2. What are the main policy differences between the Democratic candidates for U.S. Senate on the August 4 ballot?
3. On healthcare, Israel and Gaza, and immigration, how do Abdul El-Sayed and Haley Stevens differ in the Democratic Senate primary?
4. What are the main platform differences between the Republican candidates for governor, Mike Cox, John James, Perry Johnson, and Aric Nesbitt?
5. What does Jocelyn Benson's platform for governor focus on, and how's it different from Chris Swanson's?
