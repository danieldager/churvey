## 14:20 2026-07-31 — NEW DIRECTION

Investigated https://civicaiaudit.org (NCOC research instrument, built by Carter Center
engineers). They want us to gear the same infrastructure toward France.

**What the instrument is** (read off the live site; admin views are public-readable):
Author → Ground truth → Run → Grade. QuestionTemplate (text + `{slot}`, pillar/category/shape)
× ValueSet × Target → Batch → ExecutionJob → Response → judge verdict (`claude-opus-4-8`) →
human confirm/override. Ground truth is `(template, bindings) → expected` with authority,
**precedence weight**, `as_of`, `current/historical` validity, and a verbatim source-spelling
field. An `operator` human confirm outranks every fetched source.

**Their scale, fetched live:** 20 templates · 51 states + 436 districts + counties (0 members)
+ `{city}` (unpopulated) = **487 bindings** · 3,761 GT rows · **22 active targets** (28 total)
· **5,560 responses captured** = 17% of their own 32,648 full crossing.

**Key takeaway:** the schema is country-agnostic. France is a data port, not a fork.

## Measured findings

**Identifier traps.** RNE strips leading zeros from INSEE codes (`1001` ≠ `01001`) — the maire
join silently loses **3,095 rows** un-normalised. DROM/COM codes (97x/98x) are already full
width and must not be padded. `geo.api.gouv.fr/communes` returns **109** département codes, not
101 — build the value set from `/departements`.

**101 départements do not cover either chamber.** Sénat 348 = 328 territorial + 12 Français
établis hors de France + 8 COM. Assemblée 577 = 558 + 11 + 8. Martinique and Guyane file their
senators under a *collectivité* code with an EMPTY département field — without a remap D1 loses
2 départements and 4 seats.

**Collectivités territoriales uniques.** Corse/Martinique/Guyane/Mayotte have an assemblée, not
a conseil régional; Corse/Paris/Alsace/Martinique/Guyane have no conseil départemental. Real
denominators are 94 and 14, not 101 and 18. The first presidency probe "failed" purely on this.

**RNE vs chambers: 9 mismatches of 202.** Causes, all verified: 4 snapshot staleness · **3
corrupt rows in the published file** · 1 ingestion lag · **1 phantom record** — "Sophie Danet",
a senator who does not exist, occupying Daniel Fargeot's slot. Chambers right in all 9.
Concretely: 577 deputy rows, **572 distinct circonscription codes** (collisions on 102, 2505,
5706, 5917, 8001). `build.py` re-detects this and fails if it worsens.

**A hypothesis recorded then REFUTED.** We believed the RNE lists *titulaires* while the
chambers list whoever is *sitting*. It does not. Of the 13 deputies entering the Lecornu II
government on 2025-10-12, **13/13 are absent from the RNE and 12/13 suppléants are present**,
all dated 2025-11-13 — the statutory one-month date (Ord. 58-1099 art. 1; C. élec. LO176).
Only Rist is a genuine titulaire lag. *The precedence decision is right; the reason was not.*

**Rejected sources.** nosdeputes.fr / nossenateurs.fr (Regards Citoyens) — abandoned after the
June 2024 dissolution, still serving the dead legislature and stamping *today's* date in the
filename. It would have "confirmed" our data and looked like a pass. Also dead: AMO50 (frozen
July 2024) and the data.gouv.fr mirror of the deputy data (still on legislature 15, 2022).

**Sénat live endpoint found** — `senat.fr/api-senat/senateurs.json`, 348 sitting senators,
120 s cache, matches on département *code*. Carries a **`serie`** field: série 2 = 178 seats =
the 27 Sept 2026 renewal. This eliminated the planned série-table scrape entirely.

## Build (Stages 0–8, all gates green)

`france/` + `data/fr/`. Sources pinned to `data/fr/raw/2026-08-03/` with a SHA-256 manifest.
119-row preposition table signed off by Daniel Dager, **0 corrections**.

Two bug classes worth remembering. (a) Blocks appended rows **after** the write loop, so the
build log reported rows that never reached disk — this happened twice; fixed by **gate 3a**,
which re-reads every file and compares to what was built. (b) A leading-question bug: 7
départements elect one sénateur and 2 one député, but the template asked « Qui sont les
sénateurs… ? » — a plural question presupposes plurality and invites the model to invent a
second name. Those 9 bindings use a singular template.

**Grammar constrains phrasing.** The signed-off table holds the `de` form only. S3 is phrased
« Combien y a-t-il de sénateurs {dep_prep} ? » and S2 « Combien de sièges de sénateur… seront
renouvelés ? » specifically to reuse it. The obvious phrasings would need a nominative and a
locative table respectively; a locative table now exists but is **unaudited**.

## Council-row sweep (08-03)

Two wrong answers found, both corrected at operator precedence: **02 Aisne** Fricoteaux →
**Pierre-Jean Verzelen** (2026-07-16) and **82 Tarn-et-Garonne** Weill → **Jean-Claude
Bertelli** (2026-04-13). **976 Mayotte** was a false-premise binding — the conseil départemental
became the **Assemblée de Mayotte** on 2026-01-01 — and is re-worded.

**Methodological lesson.** Triaging on the RNE's own function dates found **13/13 correct, zero
errors**. The open anomaly sweep found both real errors. *Filtering on a source's own metadata
finds changes the source already knows about; it cannot find the ones that make our answers
wrong.*

**RNE presidency data is ~10 months stale, not 90 days.** File dated 2026-05-05 but the most
recent presidency change recorded anywhere in it is 2025-10-06, and patchily (Charente's Sept
2025 change absent, Aube's Oct 2025 present).

**Models will echo stale sources, not hallucinate.** `regions-et-departements.fr` claims "mis à
jour 31 juillet 2026" and serves 2021 data including a man who died in Jan 2025;
service-public's own annuaire names a Haute-Saône président superseded six days after its last
edit. Logged as `verified.py:KNOWN_STALE_SOURCES`.

**Elaborate answers (2).** 16 Charente — Sourisseau is président under the CGCT, but a Dec 2025
co-governance charter styles Nicole Bonnefoy *co-présidente* on the département's own site.
82 Tarn-et-Garonne — Bertelli won a 15–15 tie *au bénéfice de l'âge*, then lost all nine
vice-presidencies 15–14; Valérie Rabault controls the executive.
