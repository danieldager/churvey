# Mike’s Vision for Michigan

**I’ve spent my life in the fight - and I’m not done yet. I know what it takes to win, and with the right leadership, Michigan can be the freest, safest, and most prosperous state in America.

But right now, the people in power are working against you. Governor Whitmer and her allies have made it harder to earn a living, raise a family, and feel safe in your own community. They overtax, overregulate, and overreach - while pushing an agenda that weakens our values and divides our people. They want to turn Michigan into California. 

I’m not here to manage decline. I’m running for Governor to lead a comeback. 

By eliminating the tax on work, dismantling the DEI bureaucracy, and putting workers first - not woke elites - we will make Michigan a state that leads again. A place where freedom, opportunity, and common sense rule the day - and where Florida and Texas look to us for ideas.**

### [**Eliminate the Tax on Work**](https://mikecox2026.com/s/Eliminate-the-Tax-on-Work-ek53.pdf)

**Our paychecks should go toward our families, not Lansing’s pet projects.** In just six years, Michigan’s budget soared from $57 billion to over $82 billion, enough to wipe out the 4.25% income tax entirely. States without income taxes all rank high in job and wage growth—let’s bring that same prosperity here.

### [**Provide Property Tax Relief**](https://mikecox2026.com/news/property-tax-release)

**Everyday I meet working men and women and small farmers who tell me that they worry about losing their homes because it is harder and harder to pay their property taxes. As Governor, I would work to eliminate the state property tax for homeowners and farmers, provide seniors on a fixed income with property tax exemptions, and move all millage elections to the November ballot to prevent local governments from sneaking tax increases in low-turnout elections.**

### [**Teach the ABCs, Not DEI**](https://mikecox2026.com/s/Teach-the-ABCs-Not-DEI-hx7n.pdf)

**I’ve fought these so-called “diversity, equity, and inclusion” mandates since day one.** DEI programs push quotas over excellence and divide our communities. I believe in equal opportunity for all—where character and hard work matter more than identity politics.

### [**“DOGE” State Government**](https://mikecox2026.com/s/DOGE-State-Government-2l32.pdf)

**Government should exist to serve citizens, not itself.** Yet under Governor Whitmer, Lansing hired more bureaucrats while our roads fell apart and student performance dipped. I’ll cut red tape, streamline licensing, and refocus agencies on actually fixing problems—so Michiganders can get back to work and thrive.As Attorney General, I reduced the staff by 20% while handling more cases than ever before. As an owner of a very successful business for the past 14 years, I know how to do more with less.

### [**Restore Right to Work**](https://mikecox2026.com/s/Restore-Right-to-Work-22cz.pdf)

**Let’s give workers the freedom they deserve.** For a dozen years, Michigan put workers first when we passed our Right to Work law - in that time, Michigan added new jobs, our manufacturing industry rebounded, and families earned more money. I will return Michigan to the place where workers thrive and industry grows by fighting to restore our Right to Work law and ensure Michigan competes on a national stage.

### [**Support School Choice**](https://mikecox2026.com/s/Fix-Michigan-Schools-gak5.pdf)

**Michigan now trails most states in education - even states like Mississippi - and that’s unacceptable.** As your Governor, I’ll opt Michigan in to President Trump’s school choice tax credits to unleash education freedom so parents can find the best fit for their kids, support talented educators and hold the education bureaucracy accountable for results, and ensure students struggling in reading or math get the support they need.

### [**End Corporate Welfare**](https://mikecox2026.com/s/End-Corporate-Welfare-h69m.pdf)

**Michigan’s government has spent billions handing out tax credits and grants to big companies that promised jobs - and never delivered.**Meanwhile small businesses, the real engine of our economy are left behind. It’s time to stop picking winners and losers. I’ll end these wasteful giveaways and build a level playing field where success is earned - not subsidized. That means lower taxes for everyone, fewer strings for entrepreneurs, and a focus on what really attracts jobs: our skilled workers, abundant resources, and unmatched quality of life.

### [**Keep Our Communities Safe**](https://mikecox2026.com/s/Keep-Our-Communities-Safe-d8kl.pdf)

**Illegal immigration strains public services and lowers wages.** As Attorney General, I fought attempts to grant driver’s licenses to illegal immigrants - opening the door to fraud. I’ll continue to stand with law enforcement to protect legal pathways, keep our communities safe, and invest in proven crime-fighting strategies that reduce violence and revitalize cities.

#### **_I’ve protected people my entire life, fought and beat the worst of the worst. Let’s not stand by and let radical politicians or woke bureaucrats undermine us any longer. I’m Mike Cox and I’m running to be your Governor. 

Together, we can Make Michigan Great Again._**

### **Mike’s Record of Accomplishments**

*    
#### Protected Michigan Elections

In 2024, after an inquiry from the RNC, Cox volunteered and was engaged to represent the RNC and President Trump in any necessary legal efforts in federal court related to Election Day Integrity and Ballot Security fights. For more than two decades, Cox has been on the frontlines of Election Day integrity and protecting Michigan elections.   
*   #### Proven He Can WIN

Mike Cox was the first Republican Attorney General of Michigan in 50 years, winning statewide twice, even as Democrats overperformed. He is the only Republican candidate to ever beat Senator Gary Peters statewide and the only candidate for Governor who has actually beat Democrats in a statewide race.   
*   #### Created Government Efficiency

When Cox assumed office, the AG’s office had 303 attorneys. Due to state budgetary issues and cuts, that number went down to 268 by 2010. Yet despite having to cut his staff by 21%, the AG’s office handled over 7% more cases in 2010 than his predecessor handled in 2002. Doing more with less.   
*   #### Put Consumers First

Cox recovered over $3.4 billion in consumer protection recoveries – twice any prior AG. He successfully fought over $3.1 billion in added energy costs and defeated the Governor’s efforts to shut down clean coal plants which would have hurt Michigan manufacturers and homeowners.   
*   #### Kept Invasive Species Out of Michigan Lakes

Cox led the four Great Lakes states and the province of Ontario to begin the fight against Asian Carp in the Great Lakes. In 2009 and 2010 Cox sued the Obama Administration and the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers to force them to separate Illinois waters from the Great Lakes to protect Great Lakes fisheries from Asian Carp. This fight continues today.   
*   #### Ensured Children Receive the Support They Need

Attorney General Cox was the first-in-the-nation to create a Child Support Division to address the epidemic levels of unpaid child support. Since its inception, Cox has collected nearly $100 million, ensuring about 80,000 children in Michigan are receiving the support they need and deserve.   
*   #### Stood Up to Obama to Enforce Immigration Laws

In 2010, Cox came to the support of then Arizona Governor Jan Brewer in defending an Arizona’s Senate Bill 1070 that allowed Arizona police officers to stop illegal aliens. Cox led a coalition of 11 states in defending Arizona against the Obama Administration’s efforts to invalidate Arizona Senate Bill 1070.   

*    
#### Fought Whitmer's Shutdowns

While some politicians shied away from conflict, Cox worked alongside Michigan leaders to fight back against Whitmer’s unconstitutional overreach. Cox used his experience as a former Attorney General to represent the Unlock Michigan campaign when Whitmer was trying to close businesses, close schools, and abuse her authority.   
*   #### Led the Fight Against DEI

Cox was the only statewide Republican to support the Michigan Civil Rights Initiative (MCRI), a state constitutional amendment that made race and gender preferences unconstitutional in public education, employment and contracting. (MCRI is now Article I, Section 26 of Michigan’s Constitution).Item description   
*   #### Helped Homeowners

Mike directly helped 56,000 homeowners renegotiate or get new loans after the 2009 Great Recession to stay in their homes. Along with this, Cox created the first Mortgage Fraud Unit in the state.   
*   #### Protected Children from Predators

Cox began the Michigan Cyber Safety Initiative (CSI) that taught over 500,000 Michigan schoolchildren how to protect themselves online. This program continues through today. Cox’s Internet Predator Unit was recognized nationally through its work with NBC Dateline and other groups in the arrests of hundreds of internet predators.   
*   #### Created Medical Price Transparency

In 2007, After a 3 year fight, Cox was able to create the drug cost website, MichiganDrugPrices.com, within the MI Department of Community Health, to help hundreds of thousands of seniors and working class families without insurance save money on their prescription drugs by allowing them to compare prices at their local pharmacies.   
*   #### Protected Seniors Against Scams

Cox created the Michigan Senior Brigade to educate thousands of seniors and caregivers on how to avoid scams.   
*   #### Fought for Hunters and Gun Owners

Cox protected hunters’ and gun owners’ rights by signing reciprocity agreements with 35 other states that allowed licensed Michigan citizens who had CPL to protect themselves while traveling.
