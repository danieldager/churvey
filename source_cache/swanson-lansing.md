## ISSUE

# A Better Lansing

*   Audit and restructure Michigan Economic Development Corporation to make it work for everyone, not just the political insiders.
*   Reduce FOIA exemptions for government officials and increase transparency. You deserve to know what is happening with your tax dollars.
*   Create a “cooling off” period for elected officials to become lobbyists. Elected office isn’t an audition for a different job.
*   Create a Customer Service Mindset at the state level: How do we keep our people here and how do we facilitate their success through government?
