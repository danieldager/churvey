# Early Voting

​​​​​​​​Early voting is the opportunity for a voter to cast and count a ballot before Election Day. Early voting allows the voter to insert their completed ballot into a vote tabulator.​​

Note: this contrasts with voting early via an absentee ballot because absentee ballots are securely stored leading up to Election Day and then opened and counted on Election Day.

### Where will early voting take place?

At “early voting sites." An early voting site will operate similarly to an election day polling location but will be set up to serve large numbers of voters, rather than a single precinct.

*   ​​​​[Early Vo​ting Sites](https://www.a2gov.org/media/sdsd31bp/early-voting-locations.pdf "Early Voting Locations")

### Where are the early voting sites?

If you are registered in the City of Ann Arbor, you can early vote at any one of the following 4 locations:

*   [City Hall](https://maps.app.goo.gl/pNVS8gkBMyqyFVvC7), hours: 9am - 5pm

*   [Traverwood Library Branch](https://maps.app.goo.gl/b7T9xrk36HWaKk3fA),hours:11am - 7pm

*   [Malletts Creek Library Branch](https://maps.app.goo.gl/NtcPvnDx1odw5CsD6),hours:11am - 7pm

*   [Westgate Library Branch​](https://maps.app.goo.gl/vUtHWDpnqHXhLyf97),hours:11am - 7pm​

The following locations are only available during **even-year** February and November elections:

*   [UMMA](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ZygYwatZuPDXc97v7),hours:11am - 7pm​ (February and November elections only​​)

*   ​[Duderstadt Center](https://maps.app.goo.gl/5yL9QNJLdo8U7gAD8), hours: 11am - 7pm (February and November elections only​​)​

If you are registered in Washtenaw County, but not in the City of Ann Arbor, please go to the[Washtenaw County Elections Office​](https://www.washtenaw.org/3970/Early-Voting)for information.​

If you are not registered in either the City of Ann Arbor or elsewhere in Washtenaw County, please[contact your local official](https://mvic.sos.state.mi.us/Voter/Index/#yourclerk) for early voting information.

### When will early voting take place?

#### State/Federal Election Early Vote Calendar

Michigan's Constitution requires cities and townships to offer early voting in each statewide and federal election for at least 9 consecutive days, beginning on the second Saturday before the election and ending on the Sunday before the election. Early voting sites must be open for at least 8 hours each of those 9 days.

#### Special Election Early Vote Calendar

Municipalities have the option to offer Early Voting during Special Elections. The City of Ann Arbor will be conducting Early Voting for 5 consecutive days beginning the Wednesday before the election and ending on the Sunday before the election. Each Early Voting Site will be open for 8 hours on those days.

**Want to vote in-person even earlier?**Check information on[absentee ba​llots​](https://www.a2gov.org/city-clerk/elections/absentee-voting/#inpersonabsentee "Absentee Voting")for guidance. ​

### Want more information on Early Voting?

Please visit the Michigan Secretary of State's[Early Voting web page​](https://www.michigan.gov/sos/elections/voting/early-in-person-voting) for more information.
