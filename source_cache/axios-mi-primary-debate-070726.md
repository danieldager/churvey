Democratic U.S. Senate candidates Abdul El-Sayed, left, and U.S. Rep. Haley Stevens. Photos: Evan Cobb for the Washington Post via Getty Images (El-Sayed) and Sarah Rice/Getty Images

"Debate week" starts Tuesday, with candidates for governor and U.S. Senate set to square off on statewide TV ahead of the [Aug. 4 primary](https://www.axios.com/local/detroit/2026/06/24/michigan-primary-election-absentee-ballot-voting).

**Why it matters:** There are plenty of local current events to tackle — from [widespread power outages](https://www.detroitnews.com/story/news/local/michigan/2026/07/06/thousands-still-without-power-michigan-more-storms-possible-monday/90818125007/) frustrating households across Metro Detroit to the ongoing saga of the Gordie Howe International Bridge's [delayed opening](https://www.axios.com/local/detroit/2026/06/26/gordie-howe-bridge-pathway-windsor-plan-opening).

**📺 Here's the schedule:**

*   Tuesday: WOOD-TV hosts a Democratic U.S. Senate debate at 7pm, featuring Abdul El-Sayed and Haley Stevens after Mallory McMorrow [exited the race](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/05/mallory-mcmorrow-michigan-senate-stevens-el-sayed).
*   Wednesday: WJBK-TV (Fox 2) airs a Republican gubernatorial debate at 7:30pm with Mike Cox, John James and Perry Johnson, [per the Detroit News](https://www.detroitnews.com/story/news/politics/2026/07/05/michigan-debate-week-el-sayed-stevens-mcmorrow-cox-johnson-james/90782410007/).
*   Thursday: WOOD-TV returns with a second GOP gubernatorial debate featuring Cox, James and Johnson at 7pm.

**How to watch:**Debates on the [Grand-Rapids-based WOOD-TV](https://www.woodtv.com/news/elections/news-8-announces-debate-week-with-senate-gubernatorial-candidates/) will air locally on CBS Detroit.

*   They're also available to stream on woodtv.com and on the free WOOD TV+ app.

**What we're watching:** McMorrow's sudden departure leaves El-Sayed and Stevens to battle one on one.

*   How far will each candidate go to woo McMorrow's supporters?
