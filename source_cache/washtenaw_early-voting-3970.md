# Early Voting

[](https://www.washtenaw.org/3970/Early-Voting)

## [](https://www.washtenaw.org/early-voting?contentId=8b8378e1-aa10-42c5-bb78-b061788568a8)

**Michigan voters now have the right to vote early in-person during nine full days prior to every state and federal election.**

*   Voters must visit one of the Early Voting sites assigned to the city or township you live in.
*   All sites are open from the second Saturday through Sunday immediately prior to every **major** election, including on the weekends.
*   Michigan voters who have received an absentee ballot can tabulate that ballot in-person at their designated Early Voting site(s).
*   Wondering where and when you can vote early? Look up your voter record and Early Voting site information at [Michigan.gov/Vote](https://www.michigan.gov/sos/elections/voting/early-in-person-voting).
