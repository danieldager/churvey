## ISSUE

# Build in Michigan

*   Michigan built the nation, and we need to once again be a hub of economic growth, innovation, manufacturing, and entrepreneurship that has room for everyone to succeed and live with dignity.
*   No one should need to work more than 40 hours a week to make ends meet. We need to grow the economy and bring good paying, union jobs to the state. We will protect prevailing wage and worker safety.
*   Support entrepreneurship through an Office of Innovation that can support startups and small business owners through the process. Every large business starts off small, and Michigan should be the best state to start and own a business in the country.
*   I know K-Jail, not K-12. We need Career and Technical Education and trades programs in Middle School and High School so students can graduate with skilled labor into the workforce. We will also increase literacy rating by 10 points within 18 months. Reading, Financial and Digital Literacy using the EBLI model and incentivizing the home reading model.
*   We will bring down the cost of energy in the state, both by increasing energy production and by making sure DTE and Consumers only raise your rate when they can guarantee better service.
