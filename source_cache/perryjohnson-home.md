## PERRY'S PLAN

### **1. Audit every dime**

Demand accountability for every tax dollar while protecting public safety, infrastructure, and education.

[**Read more>>**](https://www.perryjohnson.com/perrys-agenda/the-mega-audit/)

### **2. End wasteful spending**

Reform state government by modernizing processes, consolidating duplicative programs, and applying performance-based management across departments.

### **3. Eliminate Michigan’s income tax**

Allow workers, families, and retirees to keep their money and live their dreams.

[**Read more>>**](https://www.perryjohnson.com/plan-to-eliminate-michigan-income-tax/)

### **4. Reform Property Tax**

Comprehensive Michigan Property Tax Reform Plan that incorporates leading reform ideas advanced in the Michigan Legislature, while going further to protect seniors.
