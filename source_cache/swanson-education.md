# 7 Point Education Plan

*   Continue Free Breakfast and Lunch Programs for ALL Michigan’s Public-School Students. This is a priority under a Swanson Administration. This allows every student the ability to get what for some may be their only nutritious meal of the day, plus help every working family in Michigan.

*   Prioritize Early Education and Literacy. Expand access to preschool and support school districts in oﬀering Universal Young 5s programs, giving students an additional year to build readiness before kindergarten. Focus on essential elementary standards so more time is dedicated to building strong foundations in reading, writing, math, and social-emotional development. Ensure every child has the early support they need to succeed in school and beyond.

*   Support Early Career Exploration and Multiple Pathways to Graduation. Create opportunities in middle and high school for students to explore careers and discover their interests. Provide meaningful learning experiences that prepare students for success after graduation. Ensure students are equipped and supported to pursue the path that is right for them, including trade school, community college, university, or military service.

*   Focus on Educator Attraction and Retention. Position Michigan as a national leader in attracting and retaining the best and brightest educators. Support and invest in educators to ensure students are prepared to succeed in a world shaped by A.I. and advanced technology. Strengthen the education profession to build a stable, high-quality workforce for Michigan’s future.

*   Protect Education Funding for K-12 Schools. Stop using the School Aid Fund to backfill shortfalls in the General Fund or to pay for unrelated priorities. School funding should be used for its intended purpose — supporting students and classrooms. Establish a bipartisan task force to review Michigan’s current school funding model and develop a fair, transparent system that reflects student needs and the true costs faced by districts.

*   No Vouchers for Private Schools. Any legislation that takes one dollar from our public schools as a giveaway to private institutions will be vetoed. Public education funding is scarce enough, and those precious dollars will not be drained from our public schools.

*   Expand GED and High School Completion Programs. Expand GED and high school completion programs through public school community education and proven models like I.G.N.I.T.E., providing adults and returning citizens with a second chance to complete their education and build productive futures. These programs help reduce re-incarceration, strengthen communities, and save taxpayer dollars. The I.G.N.I.T.E. program, founded by Chris Swanson, will serve as a model and resource to expand access and opportunity in every county across Michigan.
