# Haley Stevens Introduces Legislation To Lower Costs, Create Jobs, And Take On China To Bolster Michigan Manufacturing

7/14/2025

_[Michigan Advance](https://haleyformi.us20.list-manage.com/track/click?u=96528982c6dc7a5e7cbb95416&id=1909fb4d2b&e=03c231b19c): “The Unearth America’s Future Act was described as a means to lower costs, create jobs and ensure Michigan workers and manufacturers are leading the way in critical mineral production.”_

**DETROIT, MI** – Last week, Congresswoman Haley Stevens introduced key legislation, the Unearth America’s Future Act, which will lower costs, create jobs, and help the United States take on China by bolstering manufacturing in Michigan and across the country.
