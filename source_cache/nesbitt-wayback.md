#### Let's

Make It In Michigan.

Under Gretchen Whitmer, it has become impossible to make it in Michigan. Our economy is in decline, our kids cannot read, and our streets are less safe.

I’m running for governor because this is our last chance to save our state, and create a future where everyone can make it in Michigan.

As governor, I’ll stand with President Trump and get to work on the priorities that matter to Michiganders - cutting taxes and red tape, backing the blue to lower crime, getting DEI out of our schools, and making sure our kids don’t have to leave our state to find opportunity.

I’ve got the proven conservative record and farm boy work ethic to get the job done. With your help, we’ll win big in November, and build a future where everyone can once again make it in Michigan.

- Aric Nesbitt

#### Meet Aric

Aric Nesbitt was raised on his family’s sixth-generation dairy and grape farm in Southwest Michigan, shaping a work ethic and instilling conservative values that guide his public service.

A graduate of Hillsdale College, Aric has fought and won for conservative principles in the Michigan legislature. As the top Republican in the Michigan Senate, Aric has led the charge against Governor Whitmer– fighting the Democrats’ tax hikes, Green New Deal scheme and dangerous soft-on-crime policies.

He and his wife Trisha live just down the road from the family farm, where they are raising their three children, Catherine, William, and Margot. Aric wants to build an economy where we Make It in Michigan again.
