# What You Need to Know

Welcome to the Michigan Advance’s 2026 Michigan Voter Guide! It’s crucial to make informed choices about the candidates and issues that will shape our state’s future. This guide is designed to provide you with unbiased, essential information about each candidate in key races. Understanding these details will empower you to cast a vote that reflects your values and interests.

## Races

On Aug. 4, Michigan voters will decide the respective party nominees for a variety of races.

While Michigan Advance has highlighted the races for Governor, U.S. Senate and the U.S. House below, you can find details on all of the 110 Michigan House races [here](https://ballotpedia.org/Michigan_House_of_Representatives_elections,_2026) and the 38 Michigan Senate races [here,](https://ballotpedia.org/Michigan_State_Senate_elections,_2026) courtesy of Ballotpedia.

You can find out what is on your specific ballot through the [Michigan Voter Information Center](https://mvic.sos.state.mi.us/Home/Index).

#### Election How-To 2026

How can I get an absentee ballot?

You can apply for an absentee ballot from the Michigan Secretary of State by [Clicking Here](https://mvic.sos.state.mi.us/AVApplication/Index).

How do I register to vote?

You can register to vote via the Michigan Secretary of State by [Clicking Here](https://www.michigan.gov/sos/elections/voting/register-to-vote).

How can I check my voter registration status?

You can check your status via the Michigan Secretary of State by [Clicking Here](https://mvic.sos.state.mi.us/Voter/Index).

#### Important Dates

 June 25 

 AV ballots available for voters 

 July 20 

 Deadline to [register online](https://www.michigan.gov/sos/elections/voting/register-to-vote). 

 July 20 

 Deadline for postmark to register by mail. 

 July 24 

 Constitutionally-mandated early voting period begins. 

 August 2 

 Constitutionally-mandated early voting period ends. 

 August 4 

 Primary Day - Polls are open from 7 a.m. to 8 p.m.
